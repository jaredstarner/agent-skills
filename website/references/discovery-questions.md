# Discovery questions

Ask in numbered groups so the owner can answer inline ("1. yes, 2. b, 3. skip"). Give a recommended default for every question, and say you will drop or default anything they skip. Do not ask everything at once: groups A to D before the fit check, E to G before the build, H before launch.

## A. Purpose and audience

1. What is the site for, in one sentence? Who should find it, and what should they do after reading it?
2. Is it for you, your business, or someone else? Who approves content and design?
3. What should a first-time visitor understand within 10 seconds?
4. Are there sites you like (or dislike) for this purpose? What specifically about them?
5. Any prior notes, chats, documents, or drafts about this site? Please share them now; direction that stays in another chat is lost.

## B. Pages and content

1. Which pages? (Default for a small site: Home, About, one content section, Contact.)
2. Where does each page's content come from: you writing it, existing documents (resume, brochure, social posts), or me drafting from your notes?
3. Is anything time-sensitive (prices, availability, "now open", current role)? Time-relative claims go stale; prefer absolute facts or leave them out.
4. Is there content that must not appear (current employer, home location, family, phone number)?
5. Are you comfortable with `[TBD]` placeholders being public before launch, or should unfinished pages stay hidden? Ask this before building draft gating, which may not be wanted.

## C. Maintenance and dynamic needs

1. After launch, who updates the site, how often, and from what device? (Someone editing weekly from a phone points away from a code-based site.)
2. Do you need any of: a contact form, an email or waitlist signup, payments or a store, bookings, comments, a members area, search, a blog or feed?
3. Analytics: none, privacy-friendly and cookieless, or full tracking? Is there a rule against third-party requests? (Most analytics and embeds are third-party requests, and full tracking usually means a cookie banner.)
4. Embeds: social feeds (for example Instagram), video, maps? Each is a third-party request and can break or change without notice.

## D. Domain and hosting

1. Do you own a domain? Which registrar? Is anything live on it now, and are there existing MX (email) or TXT records to keep?
2. Apex (`example.com`) or `www` as the canonical address? (Default: apex.)
3. Is the site primarily for selling or taking payments? (This affects which hosts are allowed; see `stack-choice.md`.)
4. Repository: public or private? Private-repo hosting may need a paid plan on some hosts; check.
5. Should the site go live before content and SEO are finished, or only at the end? Ask this explicitly; owners often want to go live early, and the order of phase 7 changes if so.

## E. Visual direction (see `style-discovery.md`)

1. Light, dark, or both? Which is default?
2. Three words for the feel. Three words it must not feel like.
3. Fixed brand colors, logo, or fonts that must be used?
4. Any rules ("no animation", "minimal", "no stock photos")? For each: what is it guarding against?
5. Personal inputs that might inspire an icon or accent (a favorite animal, color, game, place), with permission to ignore them.

## F. Voice and copy

1. First person ("I build..."), no subject ("Builds..."), or third person ("Jane builds...")? Where are exceptions allowed?
2. Bullet style: start with a verb? End with a period or not?
3. Tone: formal, plain, playful? Words or claims to avoid?
4. Spelling (American or British), acronym handling, numbers as digits or words.

## G. Assets

1. Photos: who took them, may they be published, do any show children, a house, or a recognizable location?
2. Documents to publish (resume, menu, brochure)? Prepare clean exports; never publish an original working file (see `content-and-privacy.md`).
3. Logo files in vector (SVG) form? A favicon needs to read at 16px.

## H. Launch and release

1. What does "launched" mean: DNS live, announced, or indexed?
2. What does "1.0" mean: version bump, changelog, git tag, hosted release, announcement? Which of these do you want?
3. Who checks social previews (LinkedIn, Slack, X, Facebook) after launch?
